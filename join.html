How to apply

What we look for

We value curiosity, persistence, and a willingness to get your hands dirty — sometimes literally, in lake sediment. Prior research experience is helpful but not required, especially for undergraduate and early graduate positions. What matters most is genuine interest in the questions we ask and a collaborative attitude in a lab that works across disciplines.

We are committed to building a lab that welcomes people from all backgrounds. If you're unsure whether you're a fit, reach out — we're happy to talk before you apply.

  1. Read our research page to see if your interests align with ongoing projects.
  2. Email the PI with a short note about your background and what draws you to the lab, plus a CV or resume.
  3. Prospective grad students should also apply formally to the [Department Name] graduate program and name the lab as a potential advisor.
  4. We'll follow up to set up a conversation — no need to have every detail figured out in advance.
