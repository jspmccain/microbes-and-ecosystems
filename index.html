What we work on

Microbes shape ecosystems everywhere. Here's where we're digging in.

The lab's broader interest is how microbial life shapes ecosystems anywhere it's found — from the deep ocean to the human gut. Today, that curiosity is grounded in three connected areas of the water systems around us.

🌊

Aquatic Ecosystems

How microbial communities structure energy flow, nutrient cycling, and food webs across lakes, rivers, and coastal systems.

🪨

Groundwater

Microbial communities in aquifers that shape the water we pump out of the ground.
